## Description (4000 char limit)

```
CatMD is the AI cat-care app where your cat actually replies — in their own voice.

Not generic AI. Calibrated to be dry, observant, slightly imperious — closer to Co-Star than Replika in tone. Plus a daily diary the cat writes about you, personality archetype on the Feline Five, body-language read from a 6-second clip, and 60-second symptom triage with a vet-ready PDF.


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

✦ The chat IS the cat — 1-2 sentence replies in their own voice. Pinned-facts retrieval means she remembers what you've told her about herself, the family in your photos, and your shared history.

✦ Daily diary, written by the cat, about you — names your family from photos, references today's mood, specific in a way generic AI can't be.

✦ Personality archetype on the Feline Five — the only peer-reviewed cat-personality framework. Confidence sharpens as the app learns more about your cat — every check-in, body-language read, and chat feeds the read.

✦ 60-second symptom triage — for the 2 AM Google panic. Vet-ready PDF on the other side. Built to help you show up to the vet prepared, not panicked.

✦ Body-language read — upload a 6-second clip, get a paragraph in the cat's own voice on what they're saying. Multi-channel: tail, ears, eyes, posture, motion, audio.

✦ Comprehensive medical recall — the cat knows her own vaccinations, medication doses, weight history (with 90-day trend), and vet appointments. She'll even remind you when something's overdue, unprompted.

✦ Bidirectional chat — tell the cat anything. "Lily weighs 4.5 kg now" → silently logged in her profile. No forms, no banners.

✦ One-tap shareable cards — every cat reply, every diary entry, every personality result exports as a 1080×1920 card you can share with one tap.


PRICING

Free 14-day Pro trial — no card required. After: $5.99/mo or $39.99/yr. Cancel anytime.

The free tier keeps a basic version of chat, diary, and check-ins. Pro unlocks the full feature set including body-language reads, comprehensive medical recall, and the Feline Five personality archetype.


WHO IT'S FOR

Cat owners who already talk to their cat constantly — and suspect their cat understands. Owners of multiple cats. People who want to track their cat's health daily, not just at vet visits. People who want to remember what their cat was like last year, two years ago, six years ago.

Built for cats only. By cat people.

catmd.pet
```

## Submission flow (once Apple Dev approves)

1. Open App Store Connect → My Apps → CatMD
2. Paste Name, Subtitle, Promotional Text, Keywords, Description, What's New
3. Upload screenshots in the order above
4. Set Categories, Age Rating, Copyright
5. Add Support URL, Privacy Policy URL, Marketing URL
6. Click "Submit for Review"
7. Apple review takes 24-48h average for first submission (can be 5-7 days)
