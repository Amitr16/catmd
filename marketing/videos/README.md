## Folder convention (per video)

```
videos/01-chat/
├── stills/         # Nano Banana JPGs (the AI-generated reference frames)
├── clips/          # Hailuo MP4s (the AI-animated 5s video clips)
├── recordings/     # Phone screen recordings of the actual app
├── final/          # The exported final assembled video (1080×1920 MP4)
└── README.md       # Per-video shot list + status
```

Each folder is created upfront for all 9 videos so you can drop files
into any of them as you produce in parallel.

## Asset reusability

The same Lily reference photo (`marketing/assets/cat-photos/`) +
human stand-in stills are reusable across **every** video that needs
an AI clip of Lily or the owner. We're not generating 9 cats from
scratch — we're generating different scenes with the same characters.

Cost per video: ~$1-3 in fal.ai credits (Hailuo image-to-video).
Total series cost: ~$15-30 across all 9.

Screen recordings re-do per video (each shows a different app
surface) — but they're free, just your phone.

## Per-video shot lists — quick reference

### #1 — Chat
**Hook:** *"wait. she's typing?"* → owner sees reply on phone → cut back to cat unbothered → brand
**Assets:** 3 AI stills (cat at laptop / owner / cat blink) + 1 screen recording (chat) + brand card

### #2 — Diary
**Hook:** *"every night at 7pm, my cat writes a diary."*
**Single insight:** the entry is so specific it can't be a generic AI — references named family, recent days, mood arc.
**Assets:** 2 AI clips (cozy moments) + 1 screen recording (diary screen) + brand card
**Pick a diary entry with:** Bella reference / "the chair held the shape of someone who didn't sit in it" line / aristocratic-melancholic register.

### #3 — Personality Archetype
**Hook:** *"I took the cat personality quiz."* → reveal: "Velcro Cat" → cut to cat wrapped around owner's leg, accurate → brand
**Single insight:** the archetype is EERILY accurate because it's drawn from check-ins + behaviour reads, not just the quiz.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat following owner) + 1 screen recording (quiz + reveal) + 1 AI clip (cat being clingy) + brand card

### #4 — Body Language Read
**Hook:** *"I gave the AI 6 seconds of my cat. it told me she was annoyed."*
**Single insight:** the read is multi-channel and specific — Eyes / Ears / Tail / Body / Motion / Audio.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat with tail-flick + ear rotation) + 1 screen recording (body language result with new bold-channel typography) + brand card

### #5 — Daily Card
**Hook:** *"she sends me a card every morning."* → phone screen with daily card → cat reply quoted in italic serif → cut to cat in real life → brand
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat in morning sunbeam) + 1 screen recording (daily card) + brand card
**Twist option:** stitch 3 different days' daily cards to show format consistency + variety.

### #6 — Cat Studio Posters
**Hook:** *"every week my cat is somebody else."* → carousel of 4-6 posters (Cleocatra / Ghibli spirit / Mona Lily / 80s anime) → brand
**Single insight:** a NEW theme every week. Pure visual delight, share-bait.
**Assets:** 4-6 actual generated posters from the app + 1 AI clip (cat at home, normal) + brand card

### #7 — Weekly Reading
**Hook:** *"once a week, she reads ME."* → phone showing weekly reading ("you haven't been sleeping. your scent is off") → cut to owner reaction → brand
**Single insight:** the cat is the analyst — Co-Star "reading" mechanic applied to YOU.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat watching from a perch, observing) + 1 screen recording (weekly reading screen) + 1 AI clip (owner blinking at phone) + brand card
**Caveat:** needs a good weekly reading already generated in the app.

### #8 — People & Pets memory
**Hook:** *"I tagged my mom in a photo and now my cat references her."*
**Single insight:** the cat remembers everyone in your house. Memory-loop reveal.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat at door / window watching the household) + 2 screen recordings (tagging + later diary reference mentioning the name) + brand card

### #9 — Triage
**Hook:** *"I was about to call the vet at midnight. then I did this instead."*
**Single insight:** decision aid, not vet replacement. Saves the unnecessary $300 ER OR catches the real one.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat looking off, owner concerned) + 1 screen recording (full triage flow → 0-99 score → vet-ready PDF preview) + brand card
**Posts to:** TikTok + IG Reels + Reddit health-pet forums (different audience than chat).

### #10 — She told ME her shot is overdue (NEW — vc 67)
**Hook:** *"my cat just told ME her FVRCP is overdue."*
**Single insight:** the cat surfaces health knowledge UNPROMPTED — Co-Star "this app sees me too clearly" applied to vaccinations.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat watching, mildly disapproving) + 1 human reaction clip (surprise at phone) + 1 screen recording (chat exchange with overdue-vaccine seeded in Triage→Track) + 1 cat closer clip + brand card
**Pre-production:** founder must seed Triage→Track with a vaccination event whose `next_due` is in the past. Otherwise the proactive-memory line won't fire.
**See:** `NEW-FEATURES-FOR-VIDEOS.md` for full storyboard + production notes.

### #11 — Told my cat her weight. she filed it. (NEW — vc 67)
**Hook:** *"i told my cat her weight. she just… logged it."*
**Single insight:** the chat IS the data-input surface. No forms, no banners — the cat takes the fact and updates her own profile.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (owner + cat) + 2 screen recordings (chat exchange + cat-profile screen showing the auto-update) + brand card
**Pre-production:** none required — the WRITE behaviour is already in the production build.
**See:** `NEW-FEATURES-FOR-VIDEOS.md` for full storyboard.

### #12 — She knows when you skipped her dose (NEW — vc 67)
**Hook:** *"i asked my cat if she's sick. she called me out instead."*
**Single insight:** the cat tracks the HUMAN's adherence, not just her own state. Eerie + slightly judgy. Save for after audience trust is built.
**Assets:** 1 AI clip (cat near pill bottle, affronted) + 1 human reaction (caught) + 1 screen recording (chat exchange with dose-gap seeded) + 1 cat closer + brand card
**Pre-production:** founder must seed Triage→Track with a medication-dose log showing a gap (e.g. last dose 2+ days ago when reminder is daily).
**Posting cadence:** drop AFTER #10 lands. The "she's calling me out" beat is sharper if audience already trusts the cat-voice from prior videos.
**See:** `NEW-FEATURES-FOR-VIDEOS.md` for full storyboard.

```
[hook line, lowercase, conversational]

CatMD lets you talk to your cat in their voice. they reply with their personality, their memory, what you've told them about themselves.

free in beta — link in bio

#catmd #catsoftiktok #cattalk #catpersonality #catsofinstagram #aiforcats #cathumor #catlovers
```

Per-video hashtag swaps:
- **Diary:** add `#catdiary`
- **Personality:** add `#cattypes`
- **Body Language:** add `#catbehavior`
- **Daily Card / Weekly Reading:** add `#cathoroscope`
- **Cat Studio:** add `#catart #aicat`
