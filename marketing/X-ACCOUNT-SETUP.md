## Step 6 — Pinned post (the chat-screenshot card)

The pinned post is the highest-leverage real estate on the profile. It's seen by everyone who clicks through from a Reddit comment, a TikTok bio link, or a press article. **Make it the screenshot moment** — same card that drives the rest of the brand.

### Option A — chat-screenshot pinned (RECOMMENDED)

**The image:** a clean phone screenshot of CatMD chat showing one strong cat reply. Dimensions auto-handled by X. Use the same chat exchange from Video #1:

> User: "Why are you on my laptop?"
>
> Cat: "Because you keep typing instead of paying attention."

Crop tightly to the message bubbles only. Cream background. No status bar visible.

**The tweet copy (under 280 chars):**
```
i built an app where the cat replies. in their own voice. with their personality. with memory of the diary they write about you.

CatMD. android live. ios soon.

🔗 catmd.pet
```

### Option B — origin-story pinned

Longer-form, no image. Higher engagement on quote-retweets, lower on click-throughs.

```
i built CatMD because every cat owner i know talks to their cat constantly — and the cat clearly understands.

so i made the app where the cat replies. in their voice. with their personality. with memory.

solo founder. android live. ios soon.

🔗 catmd.pet
```

**Recommended:** Option A. The image makes the screenshot tangible — "this is what the app does" in 0.5 seconds. Origin-story is a great Day 1 build-in-public post but doesn't earn the pinned slot.
