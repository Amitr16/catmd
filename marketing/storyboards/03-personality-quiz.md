# Storyboard 03 — "I took a personality test for my cat"

**Goal:** BuzzFeed-style virality. Personality quizzes are TikTok catnip. CatMD has 9 archetypes mapped from real behaviour science. Format: 9:16 vertical, 20s.

**Single insight:** people share personality results. *"OMG mine is exactly that"* is one of the most-screenshotted formats on the platform.

## The 9 archetypes (for reference)

| Archetype | One-liner |
|---|---|
| Confident Sociable | Greets visitors and rules the rooms they choose. |
| Curious Introvert | Observes quietly. Forms private opinions. |
| Anxious Sensitive | Small disruptions matter. Notices edges and sounds. |
| Hunter Athlete | Focused. Movement and small prey are daily texture. |
| Affectionate Lap | Loves openly. Oriented to the human they sit with. |
| Velcro Cat | With their human, always. Tracks the day. |
| Skittish Sensitive | Watches from elevated vantage points. |
| Cool Observer | Weighs things. Measured. Judges silently. |
| Goofball Playful | Finds amusement in small disorders. |

The video works for ANY archetype — pick whichever your cat actually is, and shoot the B-roll to match.

## Capturing the quiz

1. Open CatMD → Bond → Personality tile
2. Tap "Retake quiz" if you've done it (or do it fresh)
3. Screen-record from question 1 through to the result reveal
4. Trim each question down to 1 second of footage in CapCut

## Why this lands

- People don't need to think to share a personality-test result. It's reflex.
- "Velcro Cat — she's with her human, always" is highly relatable AND specific. Either viewers feel seen (their cat IS Velcro), or curious (which one is mine?).
- The archetype reveal is **earned drama** — you watched the quiz fill in. The reveal feels like it cost you something to get to.
