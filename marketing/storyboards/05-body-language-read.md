## Capturing the body-language read

1. Film a 6-second clip of your cat doing something — tail flicking, sitting at the window, kneading, etc. Save to phone gallery.
2. Open CatMD → Bond → Read [catname] (the Body Language feature)
3. Upload the clip. Wait for the multi-channel read to land.
4. Screen-record the full flow: tap → upload → result.
5. Filter for a clip where the read is **specific and grounded** — generic results ("seems content") don't pop. Ambiguous moments where the read REVEALS something are best.

## What makes a good clip to film

- Cat sitting still but with **active body language** — tail moving, ears repositioning, eyes blinking
- Cat watching something out of frame (a bird, the door)
- Cat just after being touched / petted (transitional emotional state)
- Cat in a "what's going on" posture

## Why this works

- **Reverse-tutorial structure.** Most cat content is "look at my cat being weird." This is "I learned what my cat was actually feeling." That's a knowledge payoff people share.
- **Low-effort tryability.** Every viewer has 100 cat clips on their phone. The CTA is "upload one of yours" — no new behaviour required.
- **Science-backed credibility.** The read is structured (tail, ears, eyes, posture, audio). Looks technical. Feels validated.
