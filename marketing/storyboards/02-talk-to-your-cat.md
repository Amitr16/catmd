## Why this works

The reveal IS the value prop. Cat owners watching this immediately think: *"Wait, what would mine say?"* — and that's the click moment.

Critical: **the cat's replies must land**. If they're generic ("I'm a good cat, thank you"), the video flops. The new voice rules (post-2026-05-04) produce the right tone — but you may need 2–3 attempts to get a reply that's punchy enough for the clip. Filter ruthlessly.

## Capturing the chat

1. Set up CatMD on your phone with personality quiz completed, ideally a few weeks of usage so memory is rich
2. Start screen-recording (Android: pull down → Screen Recorder)
3. Type the first prompt slowly enough that the keyboard tap feels natural on video (the typing IS part of the content — don't paste)
4. Wait for reply. If it's not landing, swipe to clear and retry.
5. Repeat for the second exchange.
6. Stop recording. You'll trim in CapCut.

**Suggested prompts** to type (pick the 2 best replies for the 2 slots):

- "Lily, am I a good cat parent?"
- "What do you actually want?"
- "Why do you sit on my keyboard?"
- "Did you miss me today?"
- "Are you mad at me?"
- "Tell me what you think of the new vacuum."

The replies will vary; record 4–5 prompts and pick the 2 sharpest.
