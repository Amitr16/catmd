# Storyboard 01 — "Your cat has been writing a diary about you"

**Goal:** highest-priority video. Hook is unique to CatMD (no competitor has a cat-voice diary). Curiosity → reveal → smug payoff. Format: 9:16 vertical, 25–30 seconds, captions baked in.

**Single insight:** the most viral cat content lets the cat *speak*. CatMD makes that real. We deliver the punchline as text on screen, in the cat's actual voice, *from the actual app*.

## Diary entry to show on screen

Use a **REAL** diary entry generated by the app — open CatMD on your phone with at least 7 days of activity, navigate to a recent entry, screen-record it. Don't fake.

A representative one (from the production diary prompts) reads something like:

> *Saturday, the human worked from home, again. They tried to ignore me until 11am. They lost. The third attempt at the wand-toy was adequate. The chair held the shape of them after they got up; I corrected it.*
>
> *The radiator has been off three days. I have taken note.*

Pick one from your actual app. If you have a few days of generated diary entries, screen-record the **best one** — usually one with specific details about the human's day, written in the cat's snark.

## Chat reply to show on screen

For the final beat, capture this in the actual chat:
- You type: **"Lily, are you judging me?"**
- Wait for reply. With the new voice rules, expect something like: *"Obviously. Reflect on your choices."* or *"You know what you did."*

If the first reply isn't punchy enough, retry once. The new voice rules tend to land in 1-2 attempts.

## B-roll cat shots needed

You'll need 3 short AI-generated clips (Higgsfield/Seedance, 5 seconds each):

1. **Hero shot — judgmental stare** (0:00–0:02). Cat staring directly at lens, half-lit window light. Slow zoom in.
2. **Blink-cut** (0:02–0:04). Same cat, eyes close slowly, fade to black.
3. **Mild contempt** (0:14–0:18). Cat knocking something small off a table, OR sitting smugly on a closed laptop.

See `marketing/ai-video-prompts/seedance-cat-broll.md` for the exact prompts to paste into Higgsfield.

## Production notes

- **Don't over-edit.** TikTok's algorithm rewards lo-fi authenticity. Keep cuts simple. No fancy transitions.
- **Text overlay font.** Use TikTok's default sans-serif white-with-black-outline OR CatMD's brand serif (Source Serif 4) if going for a more "brand" feel. The hero text "Your cat has been keeping a diary" should be **prominent** — biggest text in the video.
- **First 2 seconds rule.** TikTok decides whether to push your video based on watch-through in the first 2-3 seconds. The hook MUST land. If the cat-stare clip doesn't grab you on its own, iterate the prompt until it does.
- **Hashtags for posting:** `#catsoftiktok` `#catmd` `#cattalk` `#catdiary` `#catpersonality` (rotate sets per post).

## Variations to A/B test

If this video lands, post these variations within a week (TikTok rewards posting cadence):

- **Variation A — same script, your own cat as B-roll** (more authentic, lower production value, but higher engagement if your cat has presence)
- **Variation B — diary entry from a "rough day"** (cat acknowledging vet visit, sad-but-dignified entry — emotional landing)
- **Variation C — empty-day entry** (the melancholic 1-2 sentence entry the cat writes when ignored — *"The day passed quietly. The chair held the shape of someone who didn't sit in it."* This is a tearjerker.)
