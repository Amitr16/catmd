# Storyboard 00 — "I can text my cat now." ⭐ LEAD (15s)

**Goal:** show the chat-as-cat interaction as the star. Single feature, single beat, single punchline. Format: 9:16 vertical, **15 seconds total**.

**Single insight:** every cat owner has imagined what their cat would say if it could talk. CatMD makes that real. The structure is **action → app reaction → reality matches** — proving the app actually knows the cat, in 15 seconds flat.

---

## Why this 15s structure works

- **0:00–0:02** is the scroll-stop. *"wait. i can text her now?"* makes a cat owner pause for the explanation.
- **0:02–0:08** is the entire demo. Question + reply. The viewer sees the actual feature work. 6 seconds of product time.
- **0:08–0:11** is the proof. Cutting back to the cat shows the reply WAS RIGHT — the app actually knows them. This is the "wait what?" moment.
- **0:11–0:15** is the brand. Short, on-screen, memorable.

15s videos have ~1.5x the completion rate of 25s on TikTok. Higher completion = more algorithmic push. We want the loop tight enough that viewers re-watch — and 15s loops feel like a single-thought hit.

### Clip 2 — Chat exchange (~6 sec, screen recording)

**Note your cat's specific behavior.** That becomes the question.

| Cat doing… | Question |
|---|---|
| On laptop | "Why are you on my laptop?" |
| At door | "Are you mad at me?" |
| At bowl | "What's wrong?" |
| Staring | "What." |
| At window | "What are you watching?" |

Open CatMD → Chat tab → screen-record while typing the question and waiting for reply.

**The reply must land.** With the new voice rules, expect punchy first-person replies:
- ✅ *"Because you keep typing instead of paying attention."*
- ✅ *"Yes. You know why."*
- ✅ *"It's warm. Also you're trying to ignore me."*

If first reply is generic (*"I just like being close to you"*), retry. Aim for **specificity + attitude**. Record 3-5 attempts and pick the punchiest. The whole video lives or dies on this reply landing.

## Production tips

### Single feature focus

We are NOT showing the diary in this video. **One feature, one punchline.** The diary gets its own 15s video (storyboard 01). Resist scope creep — every second past 15 costs algorithm momentum.

### The reply needs to be SPECIFIC

A reply like *"I love you too"* breaks the joke. The whole point is "this app KNOWS my specific cat." Filter for replies that reference the actual situation:
- Cat on laptop → reply mentions ignoring/typing/working
- Cat at door → reply mentions being shut out
- Cat at bowl → reply mentions food/hunger

If the reply is generic, regenerate the message in chat. Cost is ~3¢ per chat turn, worth iterating until it lands.

### Captions on every text moment

Every text on screen needs to be readable in 1-2 seconds. Use TikTok's "Classic" font (white with black outline) — most legible at small sizes. The cat's reply (0:04-0:08) should be the BIGGEST text in the video.

### Music

Sparse beat. No vocals. Volume at -15dB so the chat reply text reads cleanly.

Suggested style search in CapCut: "lofi minimal" / "soft hi-hat" / "calm tension." Sweet spot: a single repeating piano note + light hi-hat. Build → release on the cat-reaction beat (0:08).
