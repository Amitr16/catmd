**Goal:** show the triage / safety-net side without sounding clinical. Hook on the universal cat-owner panic moment. Format: 9:16 vertical, 20s.

## Capturing the triage flow

1. Open CatMD → Triage tab
2. Take a photo (any neutral photo of your cat will do — vet-grade behaviour around photos isn't the point of THIS video)
3. Type a believable symptom: *"threw up some white foam, otherwise seems normal"*
4. Run the scan. Wait for the result.
5. Screen-record the entire flow — scan running, result lands.

If you don't want a real scan in your history, do this on a test build / dev account. The scan output is what you're showing — the *type* of result matters more than which specific symptom.

## Why this works

- **Universal moment.** Every cat owner has been 2am-Googling at least once. The relatability is built in.
- **Anti-fear positioning.** We're not selling "freak out about your cat's health." We're selling "stop the spiral." That's the inverse of what most pet apps do — and it's why this video lands warmly, not clinically.
- **Clear value reveal.** The triage result is the payoff. Real, structured, calm. The contrast with the WebMD chaos is the joke.
