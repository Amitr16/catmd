## TikTok-native text styles

TikTok's algorithm slightly favours videos that use its native text tool (because it counts as engagement metadata). Use TikTok's built-in text editor for at least the hook overlays. Pick:

- **Font:** "Classic" (sans-serif white with thin black outline) — most legible
- **Position:** TikTok auto-suggests "safe zones" — accept them
- **Animation:** "Pop" or "Pulse" — subtle, not distracting

For the final card, you CAN replace TikTok's native text with a designed graphic exported from Figma/Canva for brand consistency. Best of both worlds.

## Caption-writing rules

The TikTok caption (200-2200 chars) appears below the video. Rules:

1. **Lead with relatability, not the product.** *"i had no idea she was journalling about me 😭"* > *"Introducing CatMD's diary feature."*
2. **Mid-caption: drop the value prop.** *"CatMD writes a daily diary in your cat's voice. it knows their personality, their archetype, the people in their life."*
3. **End with the CTA + link.** *"free during beta — link in bio"*
4. **Lowercase is fine.** TikTok rewards casual register — formal corporate-speak gets buried.
5. **One emoji max.** 😭 lands. Multiple emojis read as spam.
