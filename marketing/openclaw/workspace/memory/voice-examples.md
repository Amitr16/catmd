## ANTI-PATTERNS (auto-reject before founder review)

When generating content, if the output drifts toward any of these registers, regenerate.

### ❌ Wrong cat voice — saccharine / baby-talk

- *"OMG hooman uwu meow"*
- *"Thank you for feeding me!"*
- *"You're the best!"*
- *"Hi human! I love you so much!"*
- *"I appreciate you"* (politeness violates cat voice)
- *"You're the best"* (sentimental compliment)
- Anything ending with multiple emojis 🐾🐾🐾
- Anything with *"haha lol"*

### ❌ Wrong brand voice — corporate hype

- *"🚀 Excited to share an INCREDIBLE update!"*
- *"Discover the magic of feline AI 🐾✨"*
- *"The future of pet care is here!"*
- *"Game-changing AI for cat owners!"*
- *"Revolutionary new app..."*
- *"AI-powered next-gen feline companion!"*
- *"Get the most out of your cat parenting journey!"*
- *"Discover the secrets your cat is hiding!"*

### ❌ Wrong founder voice — corporate-speak / cringe

- *"Thrilled to announce that today we're launching..."*
- *"It's been an INCREDIBLE journey..."*
- *"Couldn't be more excited to share..."*
- *"Big news! 🎉🚀"*

When in doubt: lowercase, specific, no hype, no superlatives, no emoji-spam.

## VISUAL / DESIGN VOICE (image-generation skill cross-reference)

When generating visuals (brand cards, archetype cards, social graphics):

### ✅ Allowed
- Cream `#FAF7F2` background (dominant)
- Sage `#5B8A7A` and sage dark `#3F6456` (primary accent)
- Terracotta `#C97B63` (emotional accent — Bond pillar)
- Charcoal `#1F2024` (text)
- Source Serif 4 typography
- Generous whitespace
- Editorial / magazine-cover aesthetic
- Warm, cinematic 35mm look

### ❌ Forbidden
- Pure white backgrounds
- Pure black anything
- Loud / saturated colors
- Multiple emojis as design elements
- Gradients
- Neon
- Generic "AI-art cute" style
- "Promotional design" feel (sales-y banners, big arrows, etc.)
