## How to use this doc

1. Tier S = launch-day non-negotiables. Tier A = within first week. Tier B = within sprint. Tier C = batch in a single evening (low yield individually, stacks for SEO + tail traffic).
2. Each row has: name, URL, audience, free vs paid, expected ROI, submission effort, and the angle/hook to use.
3. **Reusable assets needed across most submissions** are listed in §6 — write them once, paste into 30+ forms.
4. **Don't submit the same day to all of them.** Apple's algorithm and PH/HN both watch for coordinated inbound — stagger over the launch week.

## ⚠️ The 2026 AI directory landscape — strategic note

The "AI directory carpet-bomb" strategy was based on early-2024 data when most AI directories accepted free submissions. **As of mid-2026, the high-traffic ones (TAAFT, Futurepedia, Toolify Pro tier) have all moved to paid-only models** — typically $49–$497 minimum. Submission-volume explosion + monetization wave + the fact that "AI app directories" are now a category competing with each other for paid placements.

**Implication for CatMD's strategy:**
- The carpet-bomb math (free × 30 sites = stacked SEO benefit) breaks when each free directory is replaced with a paywall
- Don't fight individual paywalls — assume **most major AI directories are now paid**, filter aggressively before submitting
- **Pivot the time saved** (no longer batching 15 free AI directories) into higher-yield work: creator outreach, press tier, Reddit launch posts
- The remaining **still-free directories** are the lower-traffic / community-driven ones (AlternativeTo, AI Tool Hunt, Future Tools curator) and the **indie/SaaS launch boards** (SaaSHub, BetaList, Indie Hackers, Microlaunch — verify each before submitting)

**Founder rule for hitting another paywall:** if a directory I've listed is now paid, log it in the Deferred section below and replace with the next free alternative. Don't pay any single directory more than $50 total during Pre-Launch. Revisit paid directories post-revenue.
