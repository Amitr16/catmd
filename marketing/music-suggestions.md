# Music Suggestions

Each storyboard maps to specific moods. Here's the picks per video, plus general guidance.

## Storyboard 01 — Cat Diary

**Mood:** mysterious → reveal → smug warm satisfaction

**Royalty-free options (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library):**
- "Quiet Moments" by Tony Anderson (Artlist) — solo piano, contemplative, slightly literary
- "Diary" — search this exact word in TikTok's commercial library, several lo-fi piano options match
- "Letters" by Yehezkel Raz (Artlist) — soft strings, intimate

**TikTok-trending audio (check current week):**
- Search: "cat" filtered by trending. The "I'm a cat girl" type sounds spike periodically.
- Whisper-narration trends — one of these layered behind the diary text overlay is gold.

**Avoid:** anything with vocals that compete with your text overlay. The text IS the content.

## Storyboard 02 — Talk to Your Cat

**Mood:** sparse, playful, slightly mischievous

**Royalty-free:**
- "Lo-fi Snap" — search any low-key beat with finger-snap and hi-hat
- "Quirky Whistle" tracks work for the dry comedic delivery

**TikTok-trending:**
- "Texting" sound effects (search: "texting sound")
- Notification chime from any popular app — lots of trending sounds use the iMessage notification

**Why sparse:** the cat's text replies are the punchline. Don't drown them in music. Beat-drop on the reveal of each reply.

## General music rules

1. **Volume:** music sits BELOW the spoken/text content. -12 to -18 dB under any voiceover. CapCut does this automatically with the "Audio Ducking" toggle — turn it on.

2. **Cut on the beat.** Match every visual cut to a musical beat. CapCut's "Detect Beats" feature finds them automatically.

3. **No vocal music with sung lyrics.** The lyrics will compete with your text overlays and confuse viewers reading captions on mute.

4. **Build → release.** Every 25s video should have musical build (first 15s) and release (final 10s). The release is when the brand reveal lands. The musical resolution and the brand reveal should hit together.

5. **Test on mute.** TikTok plays sound by default but a meaningful chunk of viewers watch silent. The video must work on mute (text overlays + visual storytelling) AND with sound (music + maybe voiceover). If it works on mute, it'll work with sound. The reverse isn't true.

## Where to source

- **Epidemic Sound** — paid, deepest catalogue, license clearances for TikTok/IG built-in. ~$15/mo solo plan. Worth it.
- **Artlist** — also paid, slightly different vibe (more cinematic). ~$10/mo. Pick one of these two.
- **TikTok's built-in commercial sound library** — free. Limited but usable. Filter by "Commercial sounds" if you want license safety.
- **YouTube Audio Library** — free, lower variety, but solid for emergency use.

## Sound effects

A few SFX you'll use repeatedly. Source from Epidemic / Artlist:

- **Notification chime** (for chat replies) — soft "ding"
- **Camera shutter / UI tap** — for the screen-recording app interactions
- **Slow-blink ambient** — a subtle low-pad for the diary reveal beats
- **Soft purr** — actual cat purr recordings, ~3 seconds, for closing beats
- **Foley: pen drop, keyboard tap, cup clink** — for the "everyday at home" texture
