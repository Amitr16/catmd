## 1. Title — REPLACE

**Current:** `CatMD — Your Cat, Decoded` (25 chars)

**Recommended:** `CatMD: Cat AI, Diary & Triage` (29 chars)

**Why:**
- Loads three top-intent keywords: **cat AI**, **diary**, **triage**
- Keeps brand "CatMD" as the lead element (recognisable, trademark-friendly)
- Stays under 30 chars (Play Store crops at 30 in browse views even though limit is 50)
- "Diary" is more unique-to-CatMD than "personality" (less competition in Play Store keyword graph)
- Triage anchors the medical-credibility angle for users who land via symptom search

**Alternates** (if "Triage" feels too clinical):
- `CatMD: Cat AI, Diary & Decoder` (30) — softer, but loses the symptom-search hook
- `CatMD — AI Cat Diary & Triage` (29) — same density, slightly different word order
- `CatMD: AI for Cats — Diary, Chat` (32) — leads with brand-voice but goes over 30

**Don't pick** "personality" over "diary": diary is more searchable AND more unique. Only ~12 cat apps in the Play Store reference "diary" in title or description; 100+ reference "personality."

---

## 2. Short description — REPLACE

**Current (78 chars):**
> AI that knows your cat — mood, body language, postcards, and vet-grade triage.

**Recommended (76 chars):**
> Your cat replies in their voice. Daily diary, body language, vet-grade triage.

**Why:**
- Leads with the **unique viral lever**: the cat replies in their voice. This is the line every TikTok lands on.
- "Daily diary" earns the second slot (it's CatMD's most distinctive non-chat surface).
- Drops "mood" and "postcards" from the first impression — they aren't search hooks.
- Same length, same triage anchor at end.

**Alternate:**
> Your cat replies in their voice. Diary, personality, body language, triage. (76)

---

## 3. Long description — REWRITE FIRST 3 LINES

**Current first 3 lines:**
```
CatMD is AI for cat owners.

It knows your cat — health, mood, personality, daily life — and turns that knowing into something you actually use every day. Built for cats only, trained on vet-curated feline medicine.
```

**Recommended first 3 lines:**
```
CatMD is the AI cat app where your cat replies in their voice, writes a daily diary about you, takes a personality quiz, and runs a 60-second symptom triage.

Built for cats only. Memory of named family. Body language reader. Daily card. Postcards. Movie posters.

Used by cat owners who want their cat decoded — not a generic pet app with a cat filter.
```

**Why:**
- Line 1 loads 5 keyword phrases: **AI cat app**, **cat reply**, **daily diary**, **personality quiz**, **symptom triage**. Play Store's algorithm weighs the first ~250 chars heavily.
- Line 2 covers the rest of the surface area in keyword-friendly fragments.
- Line 3 sets the positioning ("decoded, not filtered") in one line — replaces the slower "It knows your cat — health, mood…" buildup.
- Total chars added vs current: ~80. Plenty of room left under 4000-char limit.

**Keep the rest of the long description as-is** (the four-pillar 🐾 sections, WHY CATMD, disclaimer). Only the first 3 lines change.

## 4. Screenshot reorder — CRITICAL

The current order buries the chat (slot 7) and diary (slot 8) — exactly the two surfaces TikTok / IG videos will be driving traffic toward. New users who land via a viral chat-voice video and see "Today dashboard" as screenshot 1 will not connect the dots.

**Current order:**
1. `01-today-sections.jpeg` — Today (anchor)
2. `02-postcard-catsby.jpeg` — Postcard
3. `03-personality-archetype.jpeg` — Personality
4. `04-poster-catsby-hero.jpeg` — Movie poster
5. `05-body-language-result.jpeg` — Body Language
6. `06-triage-result-monitor.jpeg` — Triage
7. `07-chat-knead.jpeg` — Chat
8. `08-diary-saturday.jpeg` — Diary

**Recommended order:**
1. **`07-chat-knead.jpeg` — Chat** (lead with the unique viral lever; this is what TikTok drives traffic toward)
2. **`08-diary-saturday.jpeg` — Diary** (second hook; together with chat = "this app actually knows my cat")
3. **`03-personality-archetype.jpeg` — Personality** (Co-Star pattern; identity-driven)
4. `01-today-sections.jpeg` — Today (context / dashboard)
5. `05-body-language-result.jpeg` — Body Language (utility credibility)
6. `02-postcard-catsby.jpeg` — Postcard (visual delight)
7. `04-poster-catsby-hero.jpeg` — Movie poster (visual delight)
8. `06-triage-result-monitor.jpeg` — Triage (credibility anchor at end)

**Why this order:**
- **Slot 1** = the differentiator. "Cat replies in their voice" is what makes the install-decision.
- **Slot 2** = reinforces "this is real, not generic." Diary entry referencing named family is unfakeable proof.
- **Slot 3** = identity hook (personality archetype). Co-Star users will recognise the pattern.
- **Slots 4-7** = surface-area showcase. Order is loose; bias toward earlier slots = visual impact.
- **Slot 8** = triage. Earns the closing credibility slot. Users who scroll all 8 are doing serious eval and triage seals the deal.

**No new screenshots need to be produced** — same 8 files, just renumbered/reordered in Play Console.

## 8. Expected lift

ASO lift is hard to forecast precisely without baseline data, but conservative bands based on Phiture's case-study median:

| Change | Expected impact |
|---|---|
| Title keyword load | +15–30% organic search impressions |
| Short description rewrite | +5–10% browse-to-listing CTR |
| Long-description first-3-lines | +10–20% organic search ranking on target keywords |
| Screenshot reorder (chat in slot 1) | +20–40% listing-to-install conversion |

**Compound floor:** ~+15% organic install volume in Month 1. **Compound ceiling:** ~+50% if all four ship and the chat screenshot lands well.
