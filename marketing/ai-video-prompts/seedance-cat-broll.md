# Higgsfield / Seedance 2.0 Fast — B-roll Cat Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for AI video generation. Each block produces one 5-second clip. Use these for the B-roll moments specified in the storyboards. Mix into your edit alongside real screen-recordings of the app.

## How to use

1. **Image-to-video is preferred.** If you have a reference photo of YOUR cat, upload it as the input image — the same cat will appear across all clips, which makes the videos feel cohesive.
2. **If using stock cat photos** (no specific cat), use a different photo per shot to suggest variety, OR commit to one stock cat and reuse for consistency.
3. **Seedance 2.0 Fast** is the cheap/fast mode — good for variations. Switch to **Seedance 2.0 Pro** (or whichever full model Higgsfield offers) for the hero shot.
4. **Aspect ratio:** generate **9:16 (vertical)** if the platform allows. Otherwise generate 16:9 and crop in CapCut.
5. **Duration:** 5 seconds per clip. Most TikTok shots are 1-3 seconds, so a 5s clip gives editing room.

## Tone guidelines for ALL prompts

- **Lighting:** soft, natural. Window light, golden hour, morning kitchen, warm bedroom. Never flat fluorescent.
- **Cat behaviour:** dignified, observational. NOT silly, NOT zoomies, NOT chaotic. The CatMD brand is "aristocratic-melancholic" — the cats in our marketing reflect that.
- **Camera:** mostly static or very slow zoom/dolly. No fast cuts mid-clip — we'll cut in the edit. Cinematic, not vlog.
- **Colour palette:** cream, sage, terracotta, soft browns. Warm. Avoid harsh saturation.

## Generation tips for Higgsfield / Seedance specifically

1. **Use the "image" input** when you have a reference photo. Higgsfield's image-to-video lets you anchor the cat's appearance — same cat across all shots = pro feel.

2. **Iterate on the motion prompt.** If the first generation is too static or too chaotic, refine with: *"the cat moves only slightly,"* or *"the cat is completely still except for tail and ears."*

3. **Negative prompts to add (if Higgsfield supports):**
   `chaotic motion, fast movement, blurry, harsh lighting, fluorescent, low quality, cartoonish, multiple cats, dog, owner visible, jittery camera, glitches`

4. **Seed consistency.** If you find a seed that produces a great cat, lock it and reuse for all shots in the same campaign — reinforces character continuity.

5. **For "lip-sync" / talking-cat experiments:** Higgsfield offers character animation. You COULD make the cat appear to be "speaking" the diary lines. Start subtle — too much mouth movement reads as horror. A slight ear flick + slow blink while the diary text appears on screen is more effective than animated lip-sync.

6. **Reject 50% of generations.** AI video is hit-or-miss. Generate 4-6 takes per shot, pick the one with the most presence. Don't settle.
