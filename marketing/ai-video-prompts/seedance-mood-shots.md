# Higgsfield / Seedance — Atmospheric Mood Shots

These are NON-cat shots: kitchen at dawn, hands holding a phone, sunbeam on a chair. Use as transitions or overlays to ground the videos in the texture of "home with a cat" — gives editing room without needing 100% cat footage.

## How to use

Insert between cat clips for breathing room. 1-2 second cuts. They make the campaign feel like *life* rather than just "another pet app ad."

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 1 — "Empty Sunlit Chair"

**Use case:** transition into the empty-day diary entry. *"The chair held the shape of someone who didn't sit in it."*

```
A wooden chair in a softly-lit living room, late afternoon golden sunlight streaming across it, dust motes visible in the beam. The chair is empty but the cushion is slightly indented as if recently sat in. Camera slowly pushes forward over 5 seconds. Warm cream and amber tones. No people, no movement except the dust motes drifting in the light. Cinematic, melancholic, beautiful.
```

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 2 — "Phone on Counter at Dawn"

**Use case:** any "morning check-in" beat. The home-screen heartbeat shot.

```
A phone lying screen-up on a wooden kitchen counter, soft pale-blue dawn light from a window. The screen displays a generic "Today" home-screen interface (placeholder, no specific app branding). A coffee cup sits beside it. Slight handheld feel, intimate. The camera tilts slowly down across the scene over 5 seconds. Warm cream walls visible in the soft background. Cosy, observational.
```

**Notes:** We'll usually replace the screen content with our actual screen-recording in the edit — this shot is for the "object" feel of holding a phone.

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 3 — "Kitchen Bowl"

**Use case:** transitions in the diary video — *"the bowl was prompt"*, *"bowl mostly untouched"* etc.

```
A clean ceramic cat bowl on a tile floor, half-full of dry food, soft natural daylight from the side. Camera slowly pushes in toward the bowl over 5 seconds. The kitchen blurred behind. No movement, contemplative. Warm earthy tones — cream tile, beige bowl, brown food. Cinematic still-life feel.
```

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 4 — "Window Watcher POV (no cat)"

**Use case:** suggest the cat's vantage point without showing the cat. *"You can sense it before the humans do."*

```
First-person POV through a window: a backyard with one small bird hopping on a fence in the distance, soft afternoon light. The frame is slightly low (cat-eye level). The bird flies away. Camera is completely still, intimate, watchful. 5 seconds. Warm late-afternoon palette.
```

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 5 — "Hands Typing on Phone"

**Use case:** the "typing the chat message" beat — when you don't want to use real recorded fingers but want a generic insert.

```
Top-down close-up of two hands holding a smartphone, typing on the on-screen keyboard. Soft warm domestic light. The phone screen is partially visible but the content is illegible (placeholder). The hands are relaxed, not anxious. 5 seconds. Cream and warm wood tones in the periphery.
```

**Notes:** The actual chat content will be a screen-recording overlay; this shot is the "step back" that frames it.

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 6 — "2 AM Phone in Bed"

**Use case:** opening of the 2am-Google video — sets the time, the mood, the panic about to come.

```
Top-down shot of a person lying in bed in deep blue-purple night light, their face half-illuminated by a smartphone screen pointed at them. The phone screen is bright enough to see their concerned expression but the content of the screen is not legible. A cat's tail is visible in the lower frame, suggesting the cat is on the bed. 5 seconds, very still. Mood: late-night worry.
```

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 7 — "Closing the Phone, Exhaling"

**Use case:** payoff for the 2am video. The "back to sleep" moment.

```
A hand setting a smartphone face-down on a wooden nightstand. Soft amber bedside lamp warmth. The hand retreats out of frame. The phone is now dark, the lamp light reflecting softly on its back. Camera is static. Quiet, exhale-able, peaceful. 5 seconds.
```

---

## ATMOSPHERIC 8 — "Sun Through Curtains"

**Use case:** any seasonal / ambient transition. CatMD's diary references "the season" and "the light" — these visual moments support the diary's voice.

```
Soft morning sunlight streaming through cream linen curtains, gently moving as a breeze passes. Dust motes visible. No people, no objects in focus — just the light and the texture of the curtain. Camera completely static. 5 seconds. Quiet, contemplative, aesthetic.
```

## Mixing into the edit

A typical 25-second TikTok video uses:

- 2-3 cat B-roll shots (5s each, trimmed to 1-2s in edit)
- 1-2 atmospheric mood shots (5s each, trimmed to 0.5-1s as transitions)
- 1-2 phone screen-recordings (the actual app)
- Text overlays throughout (see `marketing/text-overlays.md`)
- 1 final "card" (still image) for the last 3 seconds

Roughly: **45% AI cat shots, 25% atmospheric, 25% real app, 5% final card.**
