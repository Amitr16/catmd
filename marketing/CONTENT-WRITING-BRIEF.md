## PART 1 — THE PRODUCT IN ONE PAGE

CatMD is an AI cat-companion app for Android (iOS follows in ~July 2026)
that does five things no other pet app does together:

1. **Your cat writes her own diary every night** — first-person, in her
   specific personality voice, referencing today's actual events,
   weather, photos, named family, and remembered objects from your
   home. Built on date-anchored grounding so a diary for last Tuesday
   never accidentally borrows today's weather.

2. **You can chat with your cat.** Not with an AI assistant. With the
   cat. Aristocratic-melancholic register, archetype-calibrated,
   memory-aware. She wakes up in a different mood every day from a
   15-mood lottery — so Tuesday's cat reads completely differently
   from Wednesday's, even though it's the same cat. Behind the voice
   sits a 4-tier quality gate that catches "your furry friend had a
   purr-fect day!" slop before it ever ships.

3. **60-second vet triage.** Photo + symptoms → 0-99 health score,
   urgency tier, differentials, red flags, and a one-tap vet-ready PDF
   export. Hard-urgency overrides for emergency keywords (lily
   ingestion, blocked urination, seizure). Backed by Merck Vet Manual,
   AAFP, ISFM, Cornell, ASPCA.

4. **Health Rhythm dashboard.** A 30-day deterministic trend reader
   over your cat's mood, appetite, weight, water, litter, and scan
   history. Drift cards flag the patterns vets actually pattern-match
   on — 3+ "off" mood days, weight ±10%, appetite "none" (anorexia →
   hepatic lipidosis in 48-72h).

5. **The cat learns who you are.** Photo tagging + chat-mention
   extraction builds a directory of named family + pets + household
   objects. Lily can reference Mom by name three weeks after you
   tagged her. The diary references the green chair you've never told
   her about — because she "saw" it in your photo last month.

The product is positioned as **"your cat decoded"** — half companion
app, half wellness companion, half (small) early-warning system for
the medical signals cats are evolved to hide.

**CatMD is the cat-care app where the cat actually replies — calibrated

### Three axes of differentiation

CatMD competes against three different product categories at once. The
positioning has to land on all three axes:

| Axis | What we're not | What we are |
|---|---|---|
| **vs generic AI chat apps** ("AI for pets" wrappers around GPT) | We're not a thin GPT wrapper that talks AS a pet | We're an architected cat voice — 15 daily moods × archetype × YOUR WORLD grounding × voice quality gate. The cat can't reference things that aren't in your actual home. |

### Category positioning

If forced to pick ONE category for app-store / press / SEO purposes:

> **"AI cat companion + wellness journal."**

Cat companion first, wellness journal second. The triage is the trust
hook — it's what makes the diary credible (we're not making things up;
this app actually understands cat health). But the daily-use surface is
the companion.

### Anti-positioning (what we explicitly are NOT)

- ❌ **Not a "cat translator."** We never claim one-to-one meow → sentence

  what your cat is saying" is over-promising.
- ❌ **Not "AI for pet owners."** Specifically cats. The product is
  species-locked. Saying "for pet owners" dilutes the moat.
- ❌ **Not "the future of vet care."** Slop-tier framing. We're
  decision support, not a vet replacement.
- ❌ **Not a chatbot.** A chatbot generic. CatMD is the cat — first
  person, archetype-shaped, memory-anchored.
- ❌ **Not a productivity app.** Despite the daily check-in habit, this

## PART 4 — STRENGTHS & UNIQUENESS

### What CatMD does that nothing else does

These are the actual moats. Every press pitch, every blog post, every
landing-page hero should put at least 2 of these in the first 3
sentences:

#### 1. **The 15-mood daily lottery — Co-Star for cats**
- 15 daily moods across 5 clusters (warm, joy, flavor, sass, dark).
- Deterministically picked per-cat-per-date using a 4-layer weighted
  lottery: `base × archMod × todayMod × feedbackMod^1.5`.
- **Same cat reads completely differently on different days.** Tuesday
  she's `attuned` (vulnerable, second-person, "you've been quiet today
  — I noticed"). Wednesday she's `imperious` ("This is my house. I let
  you live here.").
- **The user-feedback exponent is the sleeper:** the lottery learns
  which moods YOU screenshot and bends future days toward those.
  Over weeks, the cat genuinely tilts toward the voice you love.
- **No other pet app has a daily-mood system.** This is borrowed
  directly from Co-Star's daily-horoscope mechanic — applied to a cat.

#### 2. **The 15 voice modes — pop-culture cat narrators**
- One per mood. Each is a generic stylistic descriptor (legal-safe —
  we never name celebrities or shows in the prompt) that gives the
  model a SHAPE to write in.
- Examples: Wes-Anderson-deadpan (cozy), period-drama-society-narrator
  (theatrical), drag-confessional-read (roasting), sad-singer-songwriter
  (philosophical), corporate-villain-monologue (megalomania), reality-
  TV-confessional-outrage (indignant), heist-movie-voiceover
  (mischievous).
- **Borrowed from an HCI paper.** Lai, Huang, Liang (2024) found that
  factual-only cat-narrative training produces mundane voice; injecting
  a literary stylistic reference produces "deeper emotional resonance."
  Their reference was Natsume Sōseki's 1906 novel *I am a Cat*. We
  translated that insight to pop culture our actual users recognize.
- Reference: arXiv 2406.06192 — "AI Cat Narrator: Designing an AI Tool
  for Exploring the Shared World and Social Connection with a Cat."

#### 3. **The voice quality gate — slop catcher**
- A 4-tier post-generation evaluator that runs on every chat reply,
  every diary share-line, every postcard caption:
  1. Heuristic score (ban-words, generic-praise, specificity, length,
     POV, named-entity protection)
  2. If score < threshold → LLM retry with directive
  3. If retry still fails → mechanical repair (truncate, strip
     cliché)
  4. If still failing → safe-neutral fallback (small pool of
     hand-written lines)
- Banned phrases include "your furry friend," "purrfect," "I'm here
  for you," "as an AI," "I recommend." Generic praise like "today was
  wonderful" gets caught.
- **Most LLM products ship raw model output. We don't.** This is the
  single most-defensible engineering claim in our content.

#### 4. **YOUR WORLD grounding — no fictional radiators**
- Every prompt that generates voice includes a literal list of objects,
  places, weather, and people the cat has actually seen — extracted
  silently from photos (vision pass), body-language clips, and chat
  mentions.
- **The cat literally can't reference things that aren't real.** Most
  LLM cat outputs hallucinate "the radiator", "sunbeams", "Mr. Mittens"
  because the training data is full of northern-temperate generic cat
  content. Our prompts enforce: if it's not in YOUR WORLD, don't
  reference it.
- A cat in Singapore on a 32°C day will never mention "the radiator"
  by default. A cat whose owner has never tagged a "Mom" will never
  mention Mom.
- The voice quality gate enforces this from the OUTPUT side too:
  capitalized proper nouns not in the cat's `knownSubjects` allowlist
  hard-fail.

#### 5. **Live mood overlay — weather, weight, water, pain, meow**
- The daily mood lottery is the BASE. On top sits a live overlay that
  pulls today's actual signals into the voice:
  - **Weather** (Open-Meteo opt-in): thunderstorm pulls toward
    `attuned` + `indignant`. Cats hate storms.
  - **Weight trend** (30-day): `up`/`down` direction nudges toward
    `attuned` + `cozy` (the cat NOTICES a body that has changed).
  - **Water intake** (7-day baseline): low/high off-baseline → soft
    pull toward `attuned`. Only fires when there's ≥1 water log for
    the day (so "user hasn't logged yet" isn't read as "drank less").
  - **Pain score** (Feline Grimace Scale composite ≥4): hardest pull,
    overrides cheerfulness — suppresses warm/joy clusters, boosts
    dark cluster + `attuned`.
  - **Appetite off, litter abnormal, meow intent** — all flow in.
- The result: she woke up imperious, but after the thunderstorm and
  the off-baseline water and the partial appetite, she's quieter
  now. The daily base is stable, the live overlay shifts as today
  unfolds.

#### 6. **Feline Five personality framework — peer-reviewed**
- Adapted from Litchfield et al., *PLOS ONE*, 2017. They surveyed
  2,800+ cats living in homes and found 5 reliable personality factors.
- 5 archetypes: Confident-Communicator, Hunter-Athlete,
  Skittish-Sensitive, Velcro-Cat, Cool-Observer.
- 15-question quiz → archetype assignment with confidence labels
  (`forming` / `stable` / `locked-in`).
- **Every AI surface reads the archetype** for voice calibration. A
  Velcro-Cat in `imperious` mood reads differently from a
  Cool-Observer in `imperious` mood.
- We are the only consumer app shipping the Feline Five in production.

#### 7. **Feline Grimace Scale — peer-reviewed pain detection**
- Backed by Evangelista et al., *Scientific Reports*, 2019,
  University of Montreal. Validated against acute pain, chronic pain,
  postoperative pain.
- AI rates 5 facial action units (ear position, orbital tightening,
  muzzle tension, whiskers, head position) on a 0-2 scale → composite
  0-10. Composite ≥4 → consider vet examination.
- **Cats are evolved to hide pain.** Behavioral pain masking is
  evolved (they're prey animals). The FGS reads the FACE where they
  can't mask it. Genuinely high-leverage for senior / arthritic /
  post-op cats.
- Genuinely cutting-edge — the original paper has ~600 citations and
  the scale is now used in veterinary teaching hospitals worldwide.

#### 8. **Date-anchored backfill — diary that doesn't lie**
- When the diary writes about yesterday or a past day, **every
  contextual signal is anchored to that target date, not today**:
  - Weather signal: today's weather doesn't leak into a past entry
    (only fires when `deep.isToday` is true)
  - Water + weight direction: computed against THAT day's own 7-day
    baseline / 30-day window
  - Subject appearances: only counts photos taken on or before the
    target date
  - Recurring subjects: 30-day window anchored to target date
  - Vibe inclusion: only if vibe was written on or before target date
- **Most "AI journal" products time-leak.** They compute "today's
  context" then write about yesterday using it. We caught this in a
  third-party audit and fixed it in production — every diary
  backfill now reads as if written on the actual date.

#### 9. **17 audit rounds before launch — by a third-party AI**
- Production code was reviewed by Codex (a separate AI from the one
  that wrote it) over 17 read-only audit rounds.
- Each round returned structured P1/P2/P3 findings. Every finding
  was fixed and verified by 80+ fixture tests before the next ship.
- Audit findings that landed in production include the weather mood
  leak, postcard self-tagging, water-not-logged misread, weight
  signal wiring gap, emergency-tier mood routing.
- **No other indie pet app ships with this discipline.** This is a
  real moat — most AI products are vibes-coded.

#### 10. **Marketing attribution + full telemetry**
- Play Install Referrer integration ships from day one (vc 95+) —
  every install tied to its UTM source, campaign, creative.
- Typed PostHog analytics events (~100 entries) with no string typos
  possible (compile-time enforced).
- Per-LLM-call cost tracking with `cost_cents` rounded to 4 decimals —
  we know what every chat reply costs and which features bleed
  tokens.
- **Built like an enterprise product, priced like an indie one.**

## PART 6 — CUTTING-EDGE RESEARCH WE'RE APPLYING

These are the more recent, less canonical sources that inform our most
distinctive features. Cite these in tech-press and AI-engineering
content where the audience appreciates research depth.

### AI Cat Narrator (the voice-modes paper)
- **Lai, Huang, Liang. *AI Cat Narrator: Designing an AI Tool for
  Exploring the Shared World and Social Connection with a Cat.*
  arXiv 2406.06192 (2024).**
- Finding: factual-only training produces "mundane" cat-perspective
  narratives; injecting literary fictional reference (they used
  Natsume's *I am a Cat*) produces "deeper emotional resonance" and
  "more individualised personality."
- **We translated this finding** from 1906 Japanese literary fiction
  to 15 contemporary pop-culture stylistic registers (Wes Anderson,
  Bridgerton, drag confessional, etc.) — descriptors generic enough
  to be legally safe but recognizable enough to land for our
  audience.
- **First production application of their finding** as far as we
  know. The paper is qualitative work-in-progress; we shipped at
  scale.

### Defamiliarization (the literary technique)
- **Originating from Russian Formalism** (Shklovsky, 1917) — the
  practice of presenting familiar material in unfamiliar ways to
  prompt re-perception.
- Re-applied to HCI by **Bell, Blythe, Sengers (2005)** — using
  defamiliarization to design domestic technology that prompts
  reflection rather than utility.
- **We apply it as the framing for cat-perspective voice.** The cat
  doesn't describe her day the way a human would describe a cat's
  day. The framing is strange enough to invite re-reading.

### Embodied cognition / first-person voice
- **De Waal, *Are We Smart Enough to Know How Smart Animals Are?*
  (2016)** — animal cognition framing. The "bat" thought-experiment
  riff applies: we can't be a cat, but we can build something that
  invites the user to imagine the cat's interaction details.
- The first-person constraint in CatMD's chat (versus third-person
  narrator) is downstream of this — first-person reveals interaction
  details that third-person voice misses.

### Co-Star anticipation loop
- The mechanic by which a daily-changing output (horoscope, mood,
  reading) creates a daily-opening habit.
- **Adapted to 15 cat moods with a deterministic 4-layer weighted
  lottery** (base × archetype × today × user-feedback^1.5). The
  exponent on user feedback makes share-behavior the strongest
  long-term signal — the cat genuinely bends toward the moods you
  love.

### Voice quality gating
- **Drawn from a dozen smaller observations** in production LLM
  engineering — Anthropic's RLHF work on refusal-rate calibration,
  Mixpanel-style activation-event taxonomy, character.ai's voice-
  drift research.
- **Synthesized into a 4-tier deterministic gate** — evaluate, retry-
  with-directive, mechanical-repair, safe-neutral fallback. As far
  as we know, the first production cat-AI app shipping this pattern.

### Marketing attribution best practice
- **Standard mobile-attribution patterns** from Mixpanel / Amplitude
  / Adjust — unified activation event (`core_feature_used`) joined
  to install-source super-properties.
- **Implemented via Google's Play Install Referrer SDK + UTM-parsed
  super-properties registered on first launch.** No third-party
  attribution vendor (Adjust / Appsflyer) — we own the pipeline.

## PART 8 — BRAND VOICE

CatMD has two voices. The CAT voice (in chat, diary, postcards, daily
card) and the BRAND voice (on the website, in press, in store
listings, in social posts where CatMD speaks as itself).

### The cat voice

**Aristocratic-melancholic. Dry. Slightly imperious. Affection
sideways.** Voice varies by daily mood (15 moods) but the bedrock
register is consistent:

| Mood | Sample |

## PART 10 — FORBIDDEN CLAIMS & PHRASES

These are claims we cannot make either because they're not true,
because they're legally/regulatorily risky, or because they sound like
AI slop. Every piece of content must pass a final scan against this
list.

### Medical / regulatory red zone
- ❌ "Diagnoses your cat's illness" — we don't diagnose. We triage and
  flag. **Use:** "structured triage" or "decision support" or "flags
  patterns worth a vet conversation."
- ❌ "Detects [specific disease]" — we screen / flag / surface. **Use:**
  "flags early CKD patterns" not "detects CKD."
- ❌ "Replaces a vet visit" — never. We pair WITH a vet.
- ❌ "Prevents emergencies" — we surface signals you might have missed.
- ❌ "Reads your cat's thoughts"
- ❌ "Translates meows into English" — we INTERPRET; we don't TRANSLATE
  word-for-word.
- ❌ "Predicts illness" — we flag drift, which correlates with illness
  but isn't a forecast.

### AI-slop red zone (instant disqualifier)
- ❌ "Revolutionary"
- ❌ "Game-changing"
- ❌ "Cutting-edge AI" (just say what it does)
- ❌ "Smart" as an adjective for the app
- ❌ "Unlock your cat's hidden world"
- ❌ "Discover the secrets your cat is hiding"
- ❌ "The future of [anything]"
- ❌ "Powered by AI" (vague; describe what the AI does)
- ❌ Excessive emoji in copy (one is plenty; three is slop)
- ❌ "Furry friend" — banned in the product, banned in the marketing
- ❌ "Pawsome / purrfect / paw-some / paws-itively" — never
- ❌ "Cat parent" is OK; "fur baby" is not

### Internal-only / never externally market
- ❌ **The vet-confirmed outcome story funnel.** This is the internal
  capture system (Supabase `vet_confirmed_stories` + testimonial
  modal + `scan_outcome_vet_confirmed` analytics) that collects
  stories for press / case-study use. **Never reference it in user-
  facing copy, blog posts, or press.** It's the INPUT to press
  content, not press content itself. If you find a story you want to
  use, source it from the `vet_confirmed_stories_press_ready` view
  with the listed permission level honoured exactly.

### Competitive / legal red zone
- ❌ Naming specific competitors in public-facing copy (use "audio-only
  translators" or "single-channel apps" generically). Internal
  marketing docs can name names.
- ❌ Claiming first / only / best without a defensible specific
  ("the only consumer app shipping the Feline Five in production" is
  fine; "the best cat app" is not).
- ❌ Quoting any individual celebrity name in cat-voice prompt
  descriptors (use generic stylistic patterns — "period-drama
  society narrator" not "Lady Whistledown").
- ❌ Implying Anthropic / OpenAI / Google endorse CatMD. We use their
  APIs; they don't endorse us.

### Pricing red zone
- ❌ "Free forever" — there is no free-forever tier for AI features.
  The free tier has 3 scans/month and viewing of past data.
- ❌ "Lifetime access for $X" — the Lifetime tier was discontinued
  2026-05-05. Never advertise it.
- ❌ "Limited time offer" unless one is genuinely running.
- ❌ Specific dollar amounts that aren't current (Pro Monthly is
  $9.99, not $5.99 or $12.99; Pro Annual is $79.99, not $39.99 or $69).

## PART 13 — CONTENT TYPES & HOW TO WRITE EACH

### Blog post (engineering / founder-story)
- **Length:** 1,500–2,500 words
- **Audience:** Hacker News, Indie Hackers, AI engineering Twitter, AI newsletters
- **Structure:**
  1. Cold open with the problem (slop, generic AI, the 2am google
     search, etc.) — NOT with the product
  2. Show specifics (banned-word list, mood table, sample outputs)
  3. Cite research (Litchfield, Evangelista, AI Cat Narrator paper)
  4. End with one CTA (link to Play Store, or to /blog for more)
- **Voice:** founder first-person OK, plainspoken, technically honest,
  occasional sentence fragments
- **Reference example:** `proxy/blog.ts` post 1 ("Cat AI is going to
  be slop")

### Blog post (cat-care / library / SEO long-form)
- **Length:** 1,200–2,000 words
- **Audience:** worried cat owners googling symptoms / behavior
- **Structure:**
  1. Open with the reader's anxiety (cat vomited / not eating / hiding)
  2. The quick decision chart (when to see a vet vs monitor)
  3. The detailed walk-through with veterinary citations
  4. What to document before calling the vet
  5. CTA: download CatMD for ongoing tracking
- **Voice:** brand voice, plainspoken, citation-anchored, never
  alarming, never dismissive
- **Reference examples:** `proxy/library.ts` articles (cat-vomiting-
  when-to-see-vet, cat-not-eating, etc.)

### Press release
- **Length:** 400–600 words
- **Audience:** Tier 2 press (AI newsletters, Singapore tech press,
  pet-niche media)
- **Structure:**
  1. Dateline + lede (who, what, where, why-different)
  2. The three claims in one paragraph each (e.g. mood lottery, voice
     quality gate, FGS pain check)
  3. One founder quote
  4. About CatMD (boilerplate)
  5. Contact + link
- **Voice:** strict brand voice, no founder first-person except in
  the quote
- **Boilerplate:** *CatMD is an AI cat-companion app built solo in
  Singapore. It launched on Google Play in May 2026, with iOS planned
  for ~July 2026. catmd.pet.*

### Social post (X / Threads)
- **Length:** 280 chars max per post; threads up to 10 posts
- **Voice:** brand voice OR founder voice, NEVER cat voice (cat voice
  is the product; the brand is what surrounds it)
- **Structure for a one-off:** hook → specific → link or CTA
- **Structure for a thread:** hook → claim → evidence → claim →
  evidence → CTA
- **Always include:** one specific number, one specific feature, one
  link if conversion is the goal

### TikTok / IG Reels caption
- **Length:** 50–100 chars + 3–5 hashtags
- **Voice:** internet-native, current cadence
- **Hashtags:** `#catmd #cattok #aiforcats` + one trending audio-
  aligned tag
- **Don't:** name-check competitors, use "purrfect" energy, beg for
  follows

### Newsletter (founder voice)
- **Length:** 600–1,200 words
- **Voice:** founder first-person, conversational, build-in-public
- **Structure:**
  - Where we are this week (one paragraph)
  - What shipped (specifics)
  - What we learned (specifics)
  - What's next (one paragraph)
  - One ask or CTA
- **Cadence:** weekly is ideal, biweekly is fine

### Founder build-in-public post (X thread)
- **Length:** 5–10 tweets
- **Voice:** founder first-person, technically honest, specific
- **Hook patterns that work:**
  - "I shipped X in Y days. Here's the playbook."
  - "17 rounds of audit before launch. Here's what I found."
  - "Most cat AI apps are slop. Here's how we tried not to be."
- **Don't:** "I'm so excited", "thrilled", "blessed", "humbled"

### Reddit (organic, slow-burn)
- **Length:** 150–400 words for a post; 30–80 for a comment
- **Voice:** founder-as-redditor — peer, not vendor
- **Subs to lead with:** r/SideProject, r/IndieHackers (Self-Promo
  Saturday only), r/AItools, r/aiengineer; cat subs require 4 weeks
  of karma first
- **Rule:** never lead with the product. Lead with the story or the
  technical decision. Surface the product only when asked.
