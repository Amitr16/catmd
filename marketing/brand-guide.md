# Brand Guide for Marketing Videos

Quick reference. If a video drifts off-brand, check here first.

## The cat voice — non-negotiable

The cat in our marketing speaks the same way the cat in the app speaks. **Aristocratic-melancholic. Dry. Slightly imperious. Affection sideways, never gushing.**

### Allowed in scripts

- *"You're late."*
- *"The bowl is empty. Address this."*
- *"Tuna. The good kind. Don't argue."*
- *"Obviously. Reflect on your choices."*
- *"You're not the worst human."*
- *"The chair held the shape of you."*
- *"I have noticed."*

### NEVER write in scripts

- *"OMG I love you so much"* — saccharine, breaks the brand
- *"awww my hooman"* — baby talk, breaks the brand
- *"uwu meow uwu"* — never
- *"haha lol"* — never
- *"I appreciate you"* — politeness violates the cat voice
- *"Thank you for feeding me"* — never gratitude
- *"You're the best"* — never sentimental compliments

## Visual palette

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| **Cream** (background) | `#FAF7F2` |
| **Sage** (primary brand) | `#5B8A7A` |
| **Sage dark** (text on cream) | `#3F6456` |
| **Terracotta** (Bond / emotional accent) | `#C97B63` |
| **Charcoal** (primary text) | `#1F2024` |
| **Warm white** (light text on dark) | `#F2EEE6` |

Use cream as the dominant frame colour. Sage and terracotta for accents only — never as primary backgrounds. Avoid pure white, avoid black.

## Fonts

| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| **Display (final cards, big hooks)** | Source Serif 4 (download from Google Fonts) |
| **UI text / overlays** | Figtree (Google Fonts) |
| **Numbers / stats** | JetBrains Mono |

If using TikTok's built-in text tool, "Classic" is the closest approximation to Figtree. For Final-Card brand graphics, export from Canva/Figma using Source Serif 4.

## Music tone

| Mood | Style |
|---|---|
| **Diary / personality / quiet** | Solo piano, lo-fi acoustic guitar, warm strings |
| **Chat / banter** | Sparse hi-hat + finger-snap minimal beat, slightly playful |
| **2am triage** | Tense low hum → resolution to soft warm chord |
| **Body language** | Curious, unresolved (single-note repetition) → satisfaction |

Avoid: trap, EDM, anything overly polished. The brand is "thinking person who has a cat," not "viral hype clip."

## Brand voice in marketing copy (NOT the cat — the brand itself)

When the *brand* speaks (final cards, taglines, captions), the voice is:

- **Confident, plainspoken, slightly literary**
- *"AI for cat owners. Your cat, decoded."* ✓
- *"Built for cats only. By cat people."* ✓
- *"4 ways to know your cat."* ✓
- *"Free during beta. Honest pricing at launch."* ✓

NOT:

- *"The future of pet care!"* ✗ (corporate hype)
- *"Get the most out of your cat parenting journey!"* ✗ (corporate filler)
- *"Discover the secrets your cat is hiding!"* ✗ (clickbait)
- *"AI-powered next-gen feline companion!"* ✗ (over-engineered)

## Critical: the cat is the protagonist, not the app

Every video should make the **cat** the star. The app is the punchline / payoff, not the lead. If a video opens with an app screen, you've blown it.

Open with: a cat, a moment, a relatable question.
End with: the app's reveal as the "wait, what?" beat.

## Disclaimer requirements (Play / App Store)

If a video shows triage or any health-adjacent content, include a small disclaimer. Either:

- A 1-second card at the end: *"CatMD is informational. Not a substitute for veterinary care."*
- OR pin the disclaimer as a comment on the post

For non-health videos (diary, chat, personality, body language non-medical): no disclaimer needed.

## Aspect ratios + lengths

| Platform | Aspect | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | 9:16 | 15-30s | Sweet spot for our hooks. Don't go past 30s. |
| **Instagram Reels** | 9:16 | 15-30s | Same content as TikTok, post simultaneously |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 9:16 | up to 60s | Can extend a strong concept here |
| **Instagram feed (square)** | 1:1 | static + caption | For the "screenshot of cat diary" moments — easier than video |
| **Twitter/X** | 16:9 OR 9:16 | up to 2:20 | Lower priority — but the chat-screenshot screenshot format works well here |

Always master in 9:16 first. Crop down if needed for other platforms.

## Posting cadence (suggestion)

- **Week 1:** post storyboard 01 (cat diary). Two days later, post one variation.
- **Week 2:** storyboard 02 (chat) + 1 variation.
- **Week 3:** storyboard 03 (personality).
- **Week 4:** storyboard 04 (2am) and 05 (body language) — pick whichever fits the trending audio of the moment.

Cadence beats perfection on TikTok. 3-5 posts per week is the floor for algorithmic visibility.
