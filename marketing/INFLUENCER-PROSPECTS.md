## 🆕 The current offer (replaces the old "$50 + Pro lifetime" pitch)

Three parts, all stacked — **#2 and #3 apply to the annual plan only** (monthly stays at $9.99 standard, no discount, no royalty):

1. **30% revenue share** on every annual subscription redeemed with their code → **~$14 per subscriber** (after Google's 15% Play fee on the $55.99 annual price). Paid for 12 months from each user's first payment. **Settled weekly** via Stripe / PayPal / Wise. Live dashboard, every cent traceable — no "trust me" math.
2. **30% off code for their audience on the annual plan** — annual becomes **$55.99/year** (normally $79.99). Backed by the `pro_annual_partner` Play Store product, not a generic % off.
3. **Free Pro for life — for the creator and their cat.** Granted via SQL on the `pro_whitelist` table the moment they say yes. No trial timer, no card on file. Yours to keep regardless of whether their audience converts.

**Why this beats the old offer:**
- No more cash outlay per post — the partner only earns money when their audience converts (aligned incentives)
- Free Pro for life = stronger lifetime value than a one-off $50 (they keep using it)
- They become an actual product user, so content is more authentic
- Open-ended royalty window = code can keep earning for them across the full year, not just one post

## 🆕 How to grant the free Pro for life (after they say yes)

The moment they reply with their email, run this in Supabase SQL editor:

```sql
INSERT INTO pro_whitelist (email, granted_reason)
VALUES (
  'their@email.com',
  'Partner code XXXNAME30 — onboarded 2026-MM-DD. Platform handle + audience size.'
);
```

Their next sign-in with that email will flip `is_current_user_whitelisted` to true → `useEntitlement` hook unlocks Pro instantly.

### Revoking access if a partnership ends

```sql
DELETE FROM pro_whitelist WHERE email = 'their@email.com';
```

Their next session check flips them to free tier. They keep all their existing data.

## Operating notes for the founder

- **Re-verify follower counts before each DM.** Numbers above are from public-source snapshots in the last few weeks; cat-creator counts move 10–30% per quarter.
- **Avoid the top-of-funnel mega-list** (Smudge, That Little Puff, Nathan The Cat Lady, Klaus & Friends). They are above the 100K–500K caps and will quote $500–$5K flat fees that don't fit a revenue-share model.
- **Tier B prospects (rows 12–15) need a 5-minute vibe check** — watch the most recent 5 posts, confirm the voice is dry/observational not saccharine, confirm last post was within 30 days, confirm no obvious competitor partnership in the last 90 days.
- **Tier C heuristics:** the founder can convert each one into a named handle in roughly 15–20 min on the platform. Total time to fill all 8: ~2.5 hours.
- **Reply-rate expectation (updated for new offer):** at this band and offer (Free Pro for life + 30% revenue share), industry benchmarks suggest **~30–40% reply rate**, **~12–18% conversion to "yes I'll try it"**. So 20 outreach DMs ≈ 3–4 confirmed partners. Higher than the old "$50 + Pro lifetime" offer because the upside is bigger for the creator AND the no-downside framing (they keep Pro regardless) lowers the activation barrier.
- **No more `#ad` requirement for "they keep Pro for life regardless".** But once they share the code publicly with a revenue-share active, FTC `#ad` / `#affiliate` disclosure IS required — include this in your agreement template.
- **Cap initial wave at 5 partners.** Don't activate more than 5 codes in your first month. You want to A/B which creators convert before scaling — running 20 partners at once means you can't tell which creator drove what.
