## Important honesty note (read before you DM anyone)

The user constraint was clear: **"Better to have 12 reliable prospects + 8 search heuristics than 20 fabricated handles."** That is what this doc is.

- **Tier A (verified handle, verified follower band, voice fits):** 8 prospects. Names confirmed via multiple cross-referenced public sources (Catster, Favikon, Collabstr, Betches, CBC, Tubefilter, public profile pages). Follower counts are **approximate** — re-check the live profile before DM, since they shift weekly.
- **Tier B (verified handle but voice/fit needs founder eyeball before DM):** 4 prospects. Real accounts, surfaced in cat-creator directories, but we have not personally watched 30 min of their feed — founder should do a 5-min vibe check on each before sending the opener.
- **Tier C (search heuristic, NOT a handle):** 8 slots. Specific TikTok/IG search routes the founder can run in 20 minutes to surface the remaining handles themselves. We refused to make up names.

**Why the gap:** Web search returns directories that lean toward the famous (>500K) end of the cat-creator world. The 25K–75K sweet spot is exactly where directories under-index, because those creators are too small for editorial roundups and too organic for influencer-marketplace listings. This is normal — and it's why the founder's manual TikTok/IG browse in Tier C is the highest-leverage action.

## Operating notes for the founder

- **Re-verify follower counts before each DM.** Numbers above are from public-source snapshots in the last few weeks; cat-creator counts move 10–30% per quarter.
- **Avoid the top-of-funnel mega-list (Smudge, That Little Puff, Nathan The Cat Lady, Klaus & Friends).** They are above the 100K–500K caps and will quote $500–$5K. We listed three of them as OUT so they don't accidentally re-enter the funnel.
- **Tier B prospects (rows 12–15) need a 5-minute vibe check** — watch the most recent 5 posts, confirm the voice is dry/observational not saccharine, confirm last post was within 30 days, confirm no obvious competitor partnership in the last 90 days.
- **Tier C heuristics**: the founder can convert each one into a named handle in roughly 15–20 min on the platform. Total time to fill all 8: ~2.5 hours. This is the highest-leverage block of work in Week 3.
- **Reply-rate expectation:** at this band and offer ($50 + Pro lifetime), industry benchmarks suggest ~25–35% reply rate, ~10–15% conversion to "yes I'll post". So 20 outreach DMs ≈ 2–3 confirmed posts. If the founder needs 5 confirmed posts, queue ~35 DMs.
- **Compliance:** TikTok and IG both require `#ad` / `#sponsored` disclosure even for $50 + product. Add it to the brief.
