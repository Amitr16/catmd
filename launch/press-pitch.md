## Pitch email template (Tier 1/2 outlets — cat/pet journalists)

**Subject line** (pick one, A/B test):
- `New: AI vet triage — species-locked to cats (Catster fit?)`
- `Pitch: cat-only AI triage launches on Play Store`
- `Pitch: the "Duolingo for cat health triage" just launched`

**Body:**
```
Hi [first name],

Saw your recent piece on [their specific article — e.g., "litter-box changes most owners miss"]. I'm writing about something relevant to your beat.

I just launched CatMD (catmd.pet) — an AI vet triage app that's species-locked to cats. Unlike DogMD, TTcare, or generic pet apps, every condition, differential, and decision tree is feline-specific. It runs the Feline Grimace Scale pain score from a single photo, tracks sleeping respiratory rate for HCM early warning, and produces a vet-ready PDF.

Developed over 4 months with all guidance sourced from Merck Vet Manual feline sections, AAFP guidelines, ISFM, Cornell Feline Health, and ASPCA toxicity database — 527 curated knowledge cards.

A few angles I think would make a good piece:

1. The "cats hide pain" problem — veterinary research (Wiley 2025, VCA) shows cats are the hardest-masking domestic animal, and generic pet apps miss feline-specific conditions entirely.
2. Honest AI in a pet-health space full of overpromises — CatMD never calls its output a diagnosis; always cites sources; has species-lock guardrails.
3. Market-gap angle — cats are ~30% of US pet households, yet every consumer pet AI app optimizes for dog symptom patterns. CatMD is the intentional bet on the ignored half.

Happy to send over:
- Demo access + test account
- Screenshots / product imagery
- Written Q&A or a 15-min call
- Founder background + high-resolution photos

Press kit: catmd.pet/press (or I can email it directly)

Thanks for reading,
[Your Name]
CatMD
[phone] · [email]
```

**Why this template works:**
- Specific reference to their writing (60% higher open rate)
- Clear news hook (launch)
- 3 pre-baked angles (saves them work)
- Low-friction asks (demo, screenshots, short call)
- Respects their time (under 200 words)

`Trained exclusively on feline veterinary sources, launches on Google Play`

**Body:**

```
SINGAPORE — [DATE]

CatMD, an AI-powered veterinary triage assistant built exclusively for cats, launched today on the Google Play Store. The app aims to address a gap in pet-health technology: while most consumer pet symptom apps are dog-first and retrofit cat advice, CatMD's triage, differentials, and condition library are species-locked to feline medicine.

"Cats hide illness better than any other domestic animal," a CatMD spokesperson said. "A cat that has 'just been quiet' for a day may already be in crisis. That's why CatMD doesn't dilute feline advice with dog patterns — every condition, decision tree, and red-flag is feline-specific."
```

## Anti-patterns in press pitching

- ❌ **Spray and pray to 200 journalists.** They talk. Getting flagged as spammy kills future pitches.
- ❌ **Leading with "I built an app..."** — they get 50 of these/day. Lead with the angle.
- ❌ **Generic "Dear [outlet]" greetings** — name + recent article reference or delete.
- ❌ **Attachments in the first email** — hosts their spam filter. Link to press kit, don't attach.
- ❌ **Following up more than once** — establishes you as unprofessional.
- ❌ **Pitching before Production approval** — if they write it up and the app isn't live, you burn the relationship.
