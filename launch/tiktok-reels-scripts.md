# TikTok / Instagram Reels — 5 ready-to-shoot scripts

Each script is 15-30 seconds, shot-by-shot. Designed for both TikTok and Reels; caption + hashtags included.

**Format conventions:**
- `[Shot X, Ns]` = shot number + duration
- `🎙️` = voiceover
- `ON-SCREEN:` = text overlay
- `🎵` = music/audio cue

## Shooting / editing checklist

**Equipment minimum:**
- Smartphone camera (1080p is fine)
- Natural light, south-facing window
- Phone tripod or stack of books
- Lav mic if available (Shokz, Rode Wireless Go) — voiceover quality matters

**Editing:**
- **CapCut** (free) for all cuts + text overlay + music. TikTok-optimized export.
- Use **captions** on every shot. 85% of TikTok plays with sound off.
- Keep cuts fast — first 2 seconds decide whether they stay.
- No filler. Every second earns its place.

**Posting schedule:**
- 1 video / day minimum for first 7 days
- Post between 7-10pm local time (algorithm favors evening engagement)
- Same video to TikTok and Instagram Reels (maybe also YouTube Shorts)
- Don't cross-post to LinkedIn / Twitter — different audiences, write native there

**Performance benchmarks (realistic):**
- 1,000-5,000 views per video: healthy
- 10,000+ views on one: viral enough to get 5-10 tester signups
- 50,000+ views on one: can hit 20+ signups

**If nothing breaks 1,000 views in 7 days:**
- Hook is weak — try a different first 2 seconds
- Not posting at peak time — shift to 8-9pm
- Not engaging in comments within first 30 min — algorithm penalizes
