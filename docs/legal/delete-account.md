# Delete your CatMD account

_Last updated: 2026-05-13_

CatMD lets you delete your account and all associated data **without
installing the app**. This page exists so anyone — including people
who no longer have access to their phone or who never installed the
app on a new device — can request deletion.

---

## What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently remove:

- Your cat profile(s) — name, breed, weight, photo, notes
- All scans and triage results
- All body-language readings and uploaded videos
- All meow translations and uploaded audio
- All photos in your Photo Studio
- All diary entries
- All chat history with your cat
- All daily check-ins
- All learned memory items (objects, places, people, pets)
- Your account email (if you added one)
- Your anonymous user ID
- Your purchase / subscription history (the deletion does NOT cancel
  an active Google Play / Apple subscription — see below)

We do NOT retain backups of deleted data. Once processed, the data is
irrecoverable.

---

## How to request deletion

You have two options.

### Option 1 — In-app (fastest, instant)

If you still have the CatMD app installed:

1. Open CatMD
2. Tap the gear icon (Settings)
3. Tap **Forget everything about my cat**
4. Confirm

This deletes your data immediately from our servers and from your
device.

### Option 2 — Email (no app required)

Send an email to **support@catmd.pet** with the subject line
"**Delete my CatMD account**".

In the email, include **one** of the following so we can locate your
account:

- The email address linked to your CatMD account (if you added one), OR
- Your CatMD user ID — find this in the app under Settings → Account,
  OR
- A description that uniquely identifies you (your cat's name plus
  the approximate date you created the account) if you have no other
  identifier

We will:

1. Confirm receipt within 48 hours
2. Verify your identity (we may email you a confirmation link)
3. Delete your data within 7 days of verification
4. Email you when the deletion is complete

---

## What about my Google Play / Apple subscription?

If you subscribed to CatMD Pro, deleting your account does **not**
automatically cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately:

- **Android:** Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile → Payments
  & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CatMD → Cancel subscription
- **iOS:** Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CatMD → Cancel

We cannot cancel subscriptions on your behalf — only Google and Apple
can.

---

## What if I change my mind?

Deletion is final. We do not keep backups of deleted data. If you want
to use CatMD again after deletion, you'll need to start fresh — your
cat's previous diary, memories, and personality will not be
recoverable.
