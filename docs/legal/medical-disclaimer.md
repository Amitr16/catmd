# CatMD Medical Disclaimer

_Last updated: 2026-04-22_

**Please read this carefully before using CatMD.**

---

## 1. CatMD is not a veterinarian

CatMD is an **informational triage assistant**. It is **not** a
licensed veterinary service, and no part of the App — including its AI
output, its knowledge base, its urgency assessments, its health scores,
its "differentials", or its "next steps" — constitutes **veterinary
diagnosis, treatment, or advice**.

---

## 2. No veterinarian-client-patient relationship (VCPR)

Using CatMD does **not** create a veterinarian-client-patient
relationship between you (the owner) and CatMD, or between your cat
and CatMD. A VCPR, where required by your jurisdiction, must be
established with a licensed veterinarian in person.

---

## 3. AI limitations

CatMD uses artificial intelligence to interpret the information you
provide. AI can make mistakes. It can:

- **Miss serious conditions** that would be obvious to a veterinarian
  examining your cat in person.
- **Flag conditions that aren't present** based on ambiguous symptoms or
  low-quality photos.
- **Produce inconsistent or outdated information.**
- **Be confident when it should be uncertain.**

The output is generated from your text input, an optional photo, and a
curated feline medical knowledge base. It cannot replace a physical
exam, laboratory testing, or imaging.

---

## 4. When to consult a veterinarian

**Always consult a licensed veterinarian for definitive diagnosis and
treatment.** CatMD's urgency assessment is **indicative**, not
diagnostic. You should contact a veterinarian — or an emergency animal
hospital — **immediately** if your cat shows any of the following,
regardless of what CatMD says:

- Difficulty breathing, gasping, or blue/grey gums
- Collapse, unresponsiveness, or seizure
- Inability to urinate, or producing only a few drops
- Blood in urine or stool in more than a trace amount
- Suspected ingestion of a toxic substance (lily, antifreeze, human
  medication, chocolate, onion/garlic, rat poison, etc.)
- Trauma (hit by vehicle, fall from height, animal attack)
- Severe, continuous vomiting or diarrhoea
- Sudden hind-limb paralysis

**If in doubt, contact a veterinarian. CatMD should never be the reason
you delay veterinary care.**

---

## 5. Do not use CatMD for

- Diagnosing non-feline animals. CatMD is specifically trained for cats.
- Prescribing dosages for medications — **never dose any medication to
  a cat without explicit veterinary instruction**. Many human
  medications (paracetamol, ibuprofen, aspirin) are fatal to cats even
  in tiny doses.
- Making euthanasia or end-of-life decisions. These require hands-on
  assessment by a licensed veterinarian.
- Replacing routine veterinary care such as vaccinations, parasite
  prevention, dental cleaning, or spay/neuter.

---

## 6. Photos and AI image analysis

When you attach a photo, it is sent to our AI processing partner for
analysis. The analysis is based on what is **visually apparent** in the
photo and is subject to lighting, focus, framing, and resolution
quality. The App cannot magically detect disease from a photo.

---

This disclaimer applies to **every** output CatMD produces, including

Your vet decides what's actually happening and what to do about it.**
