CatMD is an **AI-powered triage and education tool for cat parents**.
It helps you:

- prepare for a veterinary visit,
- keep a private log of your cat's health over time.

CatMD does **not**:

- prescribe treatment or medication,
- replace a veterinarian,

**If your cat is experiencing a medical emergency, contact a licensed
veterinarian or an emergency animal hospital immediately.**
